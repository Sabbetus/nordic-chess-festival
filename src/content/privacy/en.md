This policy explains what personal data Nordic Chess Festival collects when you register for the festival, why, who sees it and how long we keep it.

## Who is responsible

The controller is **Nordic Chess Festival ry** (Kangasala, Finland). Contact for all privacy matters: [info@nordicchess.fi](mailto:info@nordicchess.fi).

## What we collect

When you register we collect:

- name, email address and phone number
- FIDE ID (if you have one)
- name, federation, FIDE title, FIDE rating and year of birth: if you have a FIDE ID we take these from FIDE's public rating list, so you do not type them; if you have no FIDE ID you give your name, federation and year of birth yourself (the year of birth is used to apply junior and senior prices)
- whether you ordered the bus transfer
- the amount you paid and whether the payment succeeded

The organisers can correct a name on request, for example for the entry list or your badge.

We do **not** receive or store your card or MobilePay details. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe, which gives us the payment status and the amount.

## Why we use it and on what basis

- **Running your registration and the Main Event** (confirming your entry, pairings, results, contacting you about practical matters). Basis: performance of the contract with you.
- **Taking payments and keeping accounts**. Basis: contract and our legal obligations under Finnish accounting law.
- **Publishing the player list and results.** Basis: legitimate interest and the nature of an official rated tournament; see below.

We do not use your data for marketing unless you separately subscribe to the newsletter, and we do not sell it.

## What is published

Registered players appear in the public player list and results on chess-results.com and on this website. This normally includes name, federation, title and rating. Your email address, phone number and year of birth are not published by us. Results of rated tournaments are submitted to FIDE and the Finnish Chess Federation, which publish them under their own policies.

## Who processes your data

- **Stripe** (payments).
- **Cloudflare** (hosting of this website and of the registration database).
- **Tournament software and chess-results.com** (Swiss-Manager and Chess-Results), used by the organisers and arbiters.
- **FIDE and the Finnish Chess Federation**, for rating and result reporting.
- **Google**, only if you subscribe to the newsletter through the newsletter form.

Some of these providers are located outside the EU/EEA, in particular in the United States. In that case the transfer relies on safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

- Registration details: until 12 months after the festival, then deleted or anonymised.
- Accounting records (payment amounts and receipts): 6 years after the end of the financial year, as required by law.
- Published results and ratings remain public in the archives of chess-results.com, FIDE and the Finnish Chess Federation.

## How we protect it

Registration data is stored in an access-controlled database. The organiser view is protected by a secret access key and is available only to a small number of named organisers and arbiters. All traffic uses HTTPS, payments are confirmed through signed messages from Stripe, and no card data passes through our systems. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

## Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it where we are allowed to, limit its use, or give you a copy. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to [info@nordicchess.fi](mailto:info@nordicchess.fi). If you think we handle your data unlawfully you can complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman ([tietosuoja.fi](https://tietosuoja.fi)).

## Children

Players under 18 can register. We recommend that a parent or guardian completes the registration and enters their own email address and phone number.

## Changes

We update this page if our practices change. The date of the latest update is shown above.
